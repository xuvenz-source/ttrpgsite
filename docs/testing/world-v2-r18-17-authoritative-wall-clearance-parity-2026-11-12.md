## Problem and intended contract

DM observes that a wall clipping just the outer 1–5% of a token's destination cell blocks movement. Source-real read-only PostgreSQL `forge_world_v2.step_allowed` calls `forge_world_v2.swept_hits_segment` on the entire token footprint, and direct-write/legacy motion relies on matching `forge_visibility.swept_hits_segment`. Each checks strict interior intersections. Walls are **zero-area line segments**, not filled tile percentages; the reviewed 10% solution is an **inward clearance band of 0.10 grid cell units at EACH footprint edge**. A 2x2 token still gets 0.10 grid cell per edge, not 0.20.

The sweep MUST cover the complete origin-to-destination trajectory (even 8-cell rapid drags), not merely final tile occupancy; a thin solid wall whose segment crosses the inset interior continues to block. Actual outer map bounds, all authorized scene revisions, movement identity, wall/door/window movement rules, conditional attributes and fog/light/LOS are unchanged. Server private geometry is never returned to client for prediction. A benign 5% far-edge graze permits step to x=1 against wall at x=1.95; the **next** step toward x=2 must still block. A crossing at x=1.05 remains blocked; 20% far-edge overlap at x=1.80 remains blocked.
